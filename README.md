## Hi there 👋

Calculating .me is the idea that identity is not stored — it is computed. You exist as a live, ephemeral state derived from your secret and the place you stand, leaving no automatic trace in any system. Nothing persists unless you choose to “cleak” it. This makes identity a mathematical act, not a database artifact: a self-sovereign presence that appears in real time and only becomes history by your explicit decision. In computing terms, Calculating .me is identity as a function, not a record — a precise, minimal, and powerful shift in how machines recognize a person.
